About the Artist

Women painted outside the roles built for them.

My name is Krista Graham. I’m a painter based in Phoenix, Arizona. I work across watercolor, oil, acrylic, and mixed media — on canvas, on walls, and on skin. My practice moves fluidly between surfaces. Glass, resin, and found materials enter the work when paint alone isn’t enough.

My subjects are women. Not women as symbols. Not women as archetypes or icons or figures to be decoded. Women painted free of the roles that have always said more about the culture than about her.

I’m drawn to the moment a face stops performing and just exists. To color that feels like an emotional state before it resolves into a description. That gap is where every painting starts.
